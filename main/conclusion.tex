\section{Open questions}
\label{sec:concl-open}

\paragraph{Active page-steering instead of refusal.}
\scaffold{Replace the refusal-based spec-only safety in the port with a page-steering
sealing primitive (main-only clamp + self-inverse page-bit flip on speculative-only
decoys) so no test case is discarded for page reachability; wire the spill size clamp.}

\paragraph{The unexplained prefetcher effect.}
\scaffold{Root-cause the L1D IP-prefetcher violation observed on Alder Lake, which is
deterministic in the prefetcher-enable bit.}

\paragraph{The secure / non-secure boundary.}
\scaffold{An EL3 round trip does not flush the normal-world BTB; whether the BTB
eviction channel crosses the secure boundary for a real S-EL0 victim is open.}

\paragraph{Broader precondition classes and mitigations.}
\scaffold{Further instruction preconditions to fuzz, the exact sign-bit / TBI regime
for the sign-extension primitive, and the suggested mitigation (make the speculative
treatment of legal and illegal targets indistinguishable).}
